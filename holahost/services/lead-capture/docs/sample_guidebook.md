## Welcome

Welcome to our place! This short guidebook answers the most common questions guests
ask. If you can't find what you need, send us a message any time.

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 3:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM. The building entrance code and
the apartment lockbox code are sent to you on the morning of your arrival. Please let
us know your approximate arrival time so we can have everything ready.

## Wi-Fi

The Wi-Fi network name is printed on the router in the living room, and the password
is on the welcome card on the kitchen counter. The connection is fast enough for video
calls and streaming.

## Kitchen and appliances

The kitchen is fully equipped: oven, induction stovetop, dishwasher, kettle, and a
coffee machine. Coffee, tea, salt, pepper, and cooking oil are provided. Dishwasher
tablets are under the sink.

## Heating and air conditioning

Each room has an independent climate control unit on the wall. Use the remote to set a
comfortable temperature; please turn the units off when you leave the apartment.

## Local recommendations

There is a bakery and a small grocery store within a two-minute walk, to the left of
the building entrance. Our favorite restaurant for dinner is a family-run trattoria
three blocks north. The nearest metro station is a seven-minute walk.

## House rules

No smoking indoors. Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM out of respect for
neighbors. Please take the trash to the bins in the courtyard on your way out.

## Departure

Before you leave, please close all windows, turn off the lights and climate units, and
drop the keys back in the lockbox. Safe travels, and thank you for staying with us!
